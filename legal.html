Privacy

Your offers, settings, and personal info never leave your phone. They're saved in your browser, and only you can see them.

DashCalc is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub keeps its own logs, like your IP address, and I can't see any of it. Here's GitHub's privacy statement.

DashCalc is for people 18 and older.

Got a privacy question? Ask it on GitHub Issues.

Visit counting

This is the only data we collect. We use GoatCounter, a light-weight, privacy-focused visit counter. It only counts visits to the real site, never copies of it. Here's everything it gets:

  • Page views: which page you opened, and the site you came from.
  • First visit: once ever per browser, the first time you open DashCalc.
  • First use: once ever per browser, the first time you grade an offer.
  • Active week: at most once a week, when you graded offers on 2 or more days in the last 7, or 5 or more offers in one day.

What never leaves your phone: offers, pay, miles, vehicle information, settings, ID, no user information is collected, other than strictly the usage metrics above.

The site keeps a small note of when you first use the app so we don't collect it multiple times.

The setup page contains a toggle to turn off the visit count information collected.
